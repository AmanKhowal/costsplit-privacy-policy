1. Introduction

Welcome to CostSplit: Smart Bill Splitter ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to complete transparency regarding how our mobile application handles user data. This Privacy Policy outlines our data practices for the Android application CostSplit: Smart Bill Splitter (Package: com.amankhowalhr.billsplitter).

By downloading, installing, or using CostSplit, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide (On-Device Storage Only)

CostSplit is designed with an offline-first, privacy-respecting architecture. All user-entered expense data, including:

  • Bill names, creation dates, and notes
  • Participant and attendee names
  • Itemized receipts, item names, prices, and quantities
  • Discounts, tax rates, tip amounts, and payment breakdown calculations

is stored strictly and exclusively in your device's internal SQLite database (Android Room Database). We do not transmit, sync, upload, or sell your bill information to any external server or third party. No user accounts, profiles, or passwords are created or required to use the app.

B. Information Collected Automatically via Google AdMob

To maintain the app and offer its full feature set for free, our application displays native, interstitial, and app-open advertisements powered by Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC). When the app displays ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect certain technical and diagnostic data from your device, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID) or Android ID.
  • IP Address: Used to estimate coarse location (e.g., country or city level) to serve relevant local ads and detect malicious traffic.
  • Device & Network Telemetry: Device manufacturer, hardware model, operating system version, screen resolution, mobile carrier, and network connection type.
  • Ad Interaction & Diagnostic Data: Ad viewability impressions, click interactions, app session length, latency, and crash diagnostics to prevent ad fraud and verify valid ad delivery.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information handled by or through the app is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Local Expense Computation: Calculating individual shares, proportional taxes, discounts, tips, and summaries entirely on your device.
  • Sharing via Native Intents: When you share a bill summary via WhatsApp, SMS, email, or copy to clipboard, the text is formatted locally and dispatched via Android's native share sheet.
  • Ad Delivery & Fraud Prevention: Enabling Google AdMob to display native, interstitial, and app-open ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraudulent clicks, and comply with ad delivery standards.

4. Third-Party Services & Google AdMob

Our application integrates third-party services provided by Google LLC. Specifically, we use the Google AdMob SDK (Google Mobile Ads) for native, interstitial, and app-open advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and process information in accordance with its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review Google's privacy practices:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google Uses Data from Partner Sites/Apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google AdMob Information: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or device identifiers to any data brokers or advertisers.

5. Advertising Choices & Opt-Out

You have full control over personalized advertising on your Android device. You can opt out of interest-based ads or reset your unique advertising identifier at any time:

  1. Open your Android device Settings.
  2. Scroll down and tap Google (or Privacy).
  3. Select Ads.
  4. Tap Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID (or toggle "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on older Android versions).
Note: Opting out of personalized ads does not mean you will stop seeing advertisements. Instead, you will continue to see general (contextual) ads that are not tailored to your specific interests or browsing habits.

6. Data Storage, Security, & Retention

User Data Retention: All bill records, participant entries, and splits remain in your local app sandbox. They are retained until you choose to delete them within the app, clear the application's storage data via Android Settings, or uninstall the application.

Security: Because your data resides within Android's protected application sandbox, other apps cannot access your stored bill calculations.

AdMob Telemetry Retention: Technical telemetry and diagnostics collected by Google AdMob are retained according to Google's standard data retention schedules.

7. Children's Privacy

CostSplit: Smart Bill Splitter is a general-purpose utility app intended for bill management and shared expense calculations. The app is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at amankhowalhr@gmail.com so that we can take necessary actions.
