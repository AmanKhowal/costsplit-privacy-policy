1. Introduction

Welcome to PaySplit: Easy Bill Splitter ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to complete transparency regarding how our mobile application handles user information. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding user-entered data, third-party advertising services, and in-app purchases for the Android application PaySplit: Easy Bill Splitter (Package: com.amankhowalhr.billsplitter).

By downloading, installing, or using PaySplit, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide (On-Device Storage Only)

PaySplit is built with an offline-first, privacy-respecting architecture. All user-entered expense data, including:

  • Bill names, creation dates, and notes
  • Participant and attendee names
  • Itemized receipts, item names, prices, and quantities
  • Discounts, tax rates, tip amounts, and payment breakdown calculations

is stored strictly and exclusively in your device's internal SQLite database (Android Room Database). We do not transmit, sync, upload, or sell your bill information to any external server or third party. No user accounts, profiles, or passwords are created or required to use the app.

B. Information Collected Automatically via Google AdMob

To maintain the app and offer its core features for free, the free tier of our application displays native, interstitial, and app-open advertisements powered by Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC). When the app displays ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect certain technical and diagnostic data from your device, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID) or Android ID.
  • IP Address: Used to estimate coarse location (e.g., country or city level) to serve relevant local ads and detect malicious traffic.
  • Device & Network Telemetry: Device manufacturer, hardware model, operating system version, screen resolution, mobile carrier, and network connection type.
  • Ad Interaction & Diagnostic Data: Ad viewability impressions, click interactions, app session length, latency, and crash diagnostics to prevent ad fraud and verify valid ad delivery.

C. In-App Purchase & Subscription Data

When you purchase or subscribe to PaySplit PRO, all financial transactions and payment processing are handled entirely by Google Play via the official Google Play Billing Library. We never collect, process, or store your credit/debit card numbers, bank account details, or billing addresses. The application only receives a digital purchase token from Google Play to verify your PRO entitlement.

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

PaySplit PRO provides an optional, completely ad-free premium experience with verified PRO status.

PaySplit offers the following optional digital plans via Google Play Billing:

  • Monthly Subscription (paysplit_pro_monthly): Auto-renewing monthly subscription for ad-free experience.
  • Yearly Subscription (paysplit_pro_yearly): Auto-renewing yearly subscription offering annual savings.
  • Lifetime Access (paysplit_pro_lifetime): One-time non-consumable in-app purchase granting permanent ad-free access.

Payment Processing & Security

All billing transactions are processed securely by Google Play (Google LLC) under Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. The developer does not hold or manage your payment credentials.

Subscription Management & Cancellation

You have full control over your subscriptions. You can cancel, pause, or switch your subscription at any time:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your profile icon in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  4. Select PaySplit and tap Cancel subscription.

Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. You will continue to have PRO ad-free access until the end of that paid period.

100% Ad-Free Experience

Once an active PRO purchase or subscription is detected, all Google AdMob banner, native, interstitial, and app-open ads are completely disabled across the entire app.

4. How We Use Your Information

The information handled by or through the app is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Local Expense Computation: Calculating individual shares, proportional taxes, discounts, tips, and summaries entirely on your device.
  • Sharing via Native Intents: When you share a bill summary via WhatsApp, SMS, email, or copy to clipboard, the text is formatted locally and dispatched via Android's native share sheet.
  • Ad Delivery & Fraud Prevention: Enabling Google AdMob to display ads in the free version, measure performance, and detect invalid clicks.
  • PRO Status Verification: Storing your purchase entitlement locally on your device so that PRO features and ad removal remain active even when offline or after restarting the app.

6. Advertising Choices & Opt-Out

You have full control over personalized advertising on your Android device:

  1. Open your Android device Settings.
  2. Scroll down and tap Google (or Privacy).
  3. Select Ads.
  4. Tap Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID (or toggle "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on older versions).
Permanent Ad Removal: You can completely eliminate all advertising from PaySplit by upgrading to PaySplit PRO at any time.

7. Data Storage, Security, & Retention

User Bill Data Retention: All bill records, participant entries, and splits remain in your local app sandbox. They are retained until you delete them within the app, clear the application storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

Security: Your data is isolated inside Android's secure application sandbox. Other apps on your device cannot access your stored bill calculations.

Payment & AdMob Records: Financial purchase receipts and ad telemetry are retained securely by Google in accordance with Google Play and Google AdMob data retention schedules.

8. Children's Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at amankhowalhr@gmail.com so that we can take necessary actions.
